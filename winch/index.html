Winch System ​

Rope winching for tow trucks. Hook onto a vehicle, wind it in and hear the winch work, with sound that pans and fades with distance and muffles inside a closed cabin.

Version 2.3.0

Requires community_bridge, ox_lib and oxmysql. No inventory items are needed.

Features ​

  • Rope winching. Attach a hook to a vehicle or a point and wind it in or out.
  • Positional winch sound. The winch is heard from where it is, quieter with distance, and muffled when you are indoors or inside a closed cabin.
  • Any tow truck. Set a winch point once per truck model in game, and it is saved for everyone.
  • Job restriction. Optionally limit the winch to specific jobs.
  • Server checked. Rope lengths are validated on the server.
