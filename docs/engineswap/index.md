# Engine Swap and Dyno

![Engine Swap and Dyno](/images/engineswap.jpg)

A dyno shop. Players drive in and pick an engine note from the catalogue. They hear it on the rev counter before
they buy it. Shops are placed in game and can be locked to a job.

::: info Version 2.0.0
Requires `community_bridge`, `ox_lib` and `oxmysql`, plus an engine sound pack. No inventory items are needed.
:::

## Features

* **Hear before you buy.** Preview any engine note on the rev counter.
* **Catalogue.** Sounds are grouped into categories, and shops choose which categories they sell.
* **Job locked shops.** Limit a shop to one job, or leave it open to everyone.
* **Gauges just work.** The rev counter and dyno readouts are driven by the vehicle, so a new sound needs no extra
  work.
* **Paid with money.** Sounds are bought through your framework, so there is no item to add.
