# Ammunation Management

![Ammunation Management](/images/ammunation.jpg)

A player owned gun shop. Staff craft weapons and stock the shelf, and customers buy from the shop rep.

::: info Version 5.0.1
Requires `community_bridge`, `ox_lib` and `oxmysql`. No custom inventory items are needed.
:::

## Features

* **Everything runs off the shop rep.** Customers talk to him to buy gear. Staff also get Manage Stock for the
  shelf and prices, and Business Management for materials and stock runs.
* **Crafting.** Staff craft weapons, attachments and ammunition from materials the business buys through supplier
  contracts. Crafting earns experience and unlocks higher levels.
* **Stock runs.** Collect supplies from random collection points around the map.
* **Categories.** Each file in `shared/categories` is one category, and new files appear in game with no other
  change.
* **Admin tools.** Place everything in game with `/ammucreator`.
