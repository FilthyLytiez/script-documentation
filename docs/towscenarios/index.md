# Tow Scenarios

![Tow Scenarios](/images/towscenarios.jpg)

A full tow company. Players take roadside call-outs, recover wrecks, chop vehicles and run their own businesses.

::: info Version 5.1.0
Requires `community_bridge`, `ox_lib` and `oxmysql`. Everything else is picked up automatically.
:::

## Features

* **Dynamic call-outs.** Jobs appear on a timer and are dispatched to tow drivers with a map blip and a dialogue
  from the stranded driver.
* **Scenarios.** Breakdowns, minor and severe crashes, two-vehicle collisions, head-on collisions, rollovers, car
  fires, battery runs, tyre changes and a train derailment.
* **Fire scenes.** Put fires out with an extinguisher before the car explodes. Fires can flare back up, and the
  fire bonus is shared between everyone who sprayed.
* **Rescue work.** Revive casualties, call the ambulance and bag the ones who did not make it.
* **Bystanders.** Pedestrians near a scene flee or stop to film.
* **Chop shop.** Strip vehicles for parts and export them for a profit.
* **Repair bay and spares shop.** Buy a battery, a tyre, a fire extinguisher or a crowbar.
* **Player owned companies.** Buy and sell tow companies, with XP, levels and rewards for drivers.
* **Admin tools.** Place everything in game with `/towcreator`.
